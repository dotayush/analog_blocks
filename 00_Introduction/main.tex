\chapter*{Preface}
I would like to begin this by stating \textbf{I have zero
formal education in electronics}. Electronics is a hobby
for me and I have grown to love it over the last few years.

The \textbf{handbook aims to be a collection of various
building blocks in electronics intended to be used as a
reference for designers/engineers} and \textbf{as as a
way share what I have learnt}. I have tried to minimize the
amount of textual theory and maximise the amount of raw
mathematics and equations. Therefore, to use this handbook
effectively you must be comfortable with reading and
understanding equations.

The \textbf{handbook does not aim comprehensive resource
for learning electronics or exhaustively cover every single
design block}. You should be familiar with basic concepts
like Kirchhoff's Laws and Control Systems before using this
handbook. A dedicated section on Theory is included in this
handbook that I feel is necessary to review every now and
then. I hope you may find this handbook useful in your
journey.
