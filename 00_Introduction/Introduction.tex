\chapter*{Preface}
There are thousands of books on Electronics that
describe the subject in great detail. However, most of
them focus on theory or practical applications where
they show how to build circuits for specific purposes.

When I started playing with electronics, I struggled
trying to search for fundamental building blocks for
building more complex circuits. This Handbook is my
attempt to gather all such circuits, categorise them
to my best ability and list down the design rules
that govern them with as little theory as possible.
This is a living document and I will keep updating
it as I learn more.

You \textbf{should be familiar with basic electronics
concepts like Kirchhoff's Laws and Control Systems
before using this Handbook}. A small amount of theory
that I think is important is included in Chapter \ref{ch:theory}.
