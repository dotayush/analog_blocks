\chapter*{Preface}
I would like to begin this by stating \textbf{I have zero
formal education in electronics}. This handbook is a collection
of my personal notes and learnings from the past 2 years
of self-study in electronics. I have learned a lot from
various books, online resources such as notes and videos.

The \textbf{handbook aims to be a collection of various building
blocks in electronics intended to be used as a reference
for designers/engineers}. I have tried to minimize the
amount of theory and maximise the amount of raw mathematics
and equations, which are useful for design and analysis.

The handbook is not intended to be a comprehensive resource
for learning electronics. You \textbf{should be familiar
with basic electronics concepts like Kirchhoff's Laws and
Control Systems before using this Handbook}. I have included
theory sections which I believe are necessary to understand
the equations and concepts in the handbook with Chapter
\ref{ch:theory}.
