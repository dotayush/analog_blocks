\chapter*{Preface}
There are thousands of books on Electronics that describe the
theoretical subject in great detail. However, Most of these
books do not focus on the practical aspects as faced by an
engineer while designing real world electronic devices.

I've repeatedly found myself going back to various books,
and resources trying to rememeber what the design equations
were for a particular building block. This is frustrating
and time consuming.

It would thus be really helpful to have a single living
resource that contains all known fundamental building blocks
along with their design equations and practical considerations.
with as little theory as possible. This Handbook is my attempt
at creating such a resource.

You \textbf{should be familiar with basic electronics
concepts like Kirchhoff's Laws and Control Systems
before using this Handbook}. A small amount of theory
that I believe is important is still included in Chapter
\ref{ch:theory}.
