\subsection{Pipeline ADC}\label{sec:adc_pipeline}

\subsubsection{Overview}
A chain of \(m\) stages, each a sample and hold, a coarse sub-ADC of
\(n_s\) bits, and an MDAC (Section~\ref{sec:dac_mdac}) forming the
amplified residue for the next stage. All stages work concurrently on
successive samples: throughput of one sample per clock, latency \(m\)
clocks.

\subsubsection{Transfer Function}
\textbf{Large Signal, Time Domain, DC Input Analysis (Q5)}, 1.5 bit stage
(\(b \in \{-1, 0, 1\}\), input range \(\pm V_{REF}\)):
\begin{align*}
  b &= \begin{cases} -1 & V_{IN} < -\frac{V_{REF}}{4} \\ 0 & -\frac{V_{REF}}{4} \leq V_{IN} \leq \frac{V_{REF}}{4} \\ +1 & V_{IN} > \frac{V_{REF}}{4} \end{cases} \\
  V_{RES} &= 2 V_{IN} - b V_{REF} \in \left[-V_{REF}, V_{REF}\right]
\end{align*}
Unrolling \(m\) stages (\(V_{RES,k} = 2V_{RES,k-1} - b_k V_{REF}\)):
\begin{align*}
  V_{RES,m} &= 2^m V_{IN} - V_{REF}\sum_{k=1}^{m} b_k 2^{m-k} \\
  V_{IN} &= V_{REF}\sum_{k=1}^{m} b_k 2^{-k} + \frac{V_{RES,m}}{2^m}
\end{align*}
so \(D = \sum_k b_k 2^{m-k}\) (an overlapping add) resolves \(V_{IN}\) to
\(\frac{V_{REF}}{2^m}\) plus a final flash on \(V_{RES,m}\).

\subsubsection{Analysis}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Redundancy}: the thresholds \(\pm\frac{V_{REF}}{4}\) can move by up
    to \(\pm\frac{V_{REF}}{4}\) and \(V_{RES}\) still stays inside \(\pm V_{REF}\); later
    stages correct the decision, so cheap comparators with large offsets
    suffice.
  \item \textbf{Gain Error}: an inter-stage gain \(2(1 + e)\) (finite op-amp
    gain, capacitor mismatch) gives an error at stage 1 transitions of
    \(e V_{REF}\) referred to the output, i.e. \(e\, 2^{n-1}\) LSB. Needs
    \(\frac{1}{AF} < \frac{1}{2^{n}}\): \(A > \frac{2^{n}}{F} = 2^{n+1}\) for \(F = \frac{1}{2}\).
  \item \textbf{Noise}: stage \(k\) noise is divided by \(2^{k-1}\) referred to
    the input, so the first stage dominates and later stages are scaled down
    in capacitance and power.
\end{enumerate}
