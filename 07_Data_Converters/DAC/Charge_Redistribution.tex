\subsection{Charge Redistribution DAC and MDAC}\label{sec:dac_mdac}

\subsubsection{Overview}
Binary weighted capacitors \(C_k = 2^k C_u\) plus a terminating \(C_u\)
(total \(2^n C_u\)) share a top plate \(V_x\). After a reset the bottom
plates are switched to \(V_{REF}\) or ground and charge redistributes.
Capacitor matching is the best of any integrated element, and no static
current flows. When the reference is replaced by an analog input the
output is the product \(V_{IN} \cdot \frac{D}{2^n}\): a Multiplying DAC (MDAC),
the core of SAR and pipeline ADCs.

\subsubsection{Transfer Function}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Large Signal, Time Domain, DC Input Analysis (Q5)}: reset
    puts zero charge on the top plate (\(V_x = 0\), all bottom plates at 0).
    With top plate parasitic \(C_p\), charge conservation after switching:
    \begin{align*}
      \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} C_k (V_x - b_k V_{REF}) + C_u (V_x - 0) + C_p V_x &= 0 \\
      V_x \left(\sum_k C_k + C_u + C_p\right) &= V_{REF} \sum_k b_k 2^k C_u \\
      V_x &= \frac{D\, C_u}{2^n C_u + C_p} V_{REF} = \frac{D}{2^n} V_{REF} \cdot \frac{1}{1 + \frac{C_p}{2^n C_u}}
    \end{align*}
    \(C_p\) is a gain error only (linearity is untouched).
  \item \textbf{Pipeline MDAC stage}: \(C_1\), \(C_2\) around an op-amp. Phase 1:
    both sample \(V_{IN}\) against the virtual ground (top plate charge
    \(-(C_1 + C_2)V_{IN}\)). Phase 2: \(C_2\) is the feedback capacitor, \(C_1\)
    switches to \(b V_{REF}\), \(b \in \{-1, 0, 1\}\):
    \begin{align*}
      -(C_1 + C_2) V_{IN} &= -C_1 b V_{REF} - C_2 V_{OUT} \\
      V_{OUT} &= \left(1 + \frac{C_1}{C_2}\right) V_{IN} - \frac{C_1}{C_2} b V_{REF} \xrightarrow{C_1 = C_2} 2 V_{IN} - b V_{REF}
    \end{align*}
    With finite op-amp gain \(A\) and feedback factor \(F = \frac{C_2}{C_1 + C_2 + C_{in}}\),
    the residue is scaled by \(\frac{AF}{1 + AF}\) (Section~\ref{sec:devices_opamp}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Analysis}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Mismatch}: as the binary current DAC,
    \(SD(DNL_{N/2}) = \sqrt{2^n - 1}\, S_C\).
  \item \textbf{Area}: \(2^n C_u\) grows fast; a bridge (split) capacitor
    \(C_b = \frac{2^{m}}{2^{m} - 1} C_u\) between an \(m\)-bit LSB array and the MSB
    array cuts the total to \(\approx (2^{n-m} + 2^m) C_u\).
  \item \textbf{kT/C}: sampling on \(C_{tot}\) adds noise
    \(\frac{kT}{C_{tot}}\) (Section~\ref{sec:theory_general_noise}); it must stay
    below \(\frac{X_{LSB}^2}{12}\), i.e. \(C_{tot} > \frac{12 kT 2^{2n}}{V_{REF}^2}\).
\end{enumerate}
