\chapter{Data Converters}\label{ch:data_converters}

A data converter moves a signal between the continuous (analog) and the
discrete (digital) world. A Digital to Analog Converter (DAC) maps an
\(n\)-bit code onto one of \(2^n\) analog levels. An Analog to Digital
Converter (ADC) maps an analog level onto the \(n\)-bit code whose level
is closest to it.

\section{Analog to Digital Converters (ADC)}\label{sec:adc}
Nyquist rate converters (\(f_s \geq 2 f_{in,\max}\), one code per sample):
Flash, Two/Multi-Step Flash, Pipeline, Cyclic, SAR, Interpolating,
Folding, Dual Slope. Over-sampled converters (\(f_s \gg 2 f_B\), resolution
from averaging and noise shaping): Delta-Sigma of first or higher order,
single or multi-bit, discrete or continuous time.
