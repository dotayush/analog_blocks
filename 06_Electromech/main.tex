\chapter{Electromechanics}\label{ch:electromechanics}

This chapter covers electromechanical devices, which are devices that
convert electrical energy into other useful energy or vice versa. These
devices are widely used in various applications, including robotics,
automation, and transportation.

We try to exploit the relationship between electricity and magnetism
which is then translated into mechanical action or vice versa. For example,
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A motor converts electrical energy into magnetic energy, which
    is then converted into mechanical energy in the form of rotational
    motion.
  \item A generator converts mechanical energy into magnetic energy,
    which is then converted into electrical energy.
  \item A transformer converts electrical energy at one voltage level
    into electrical energy at another voltage level by exploiting
    magnetic coupling between two coils of wire.
  \item A relay uses an electromagnet to mechanically switch a
    circuit on or off.
  \item A solenoid uses an electromagnet to create linear motion,
    which can be used to actuate a mechanical device.
\end{enumerate}
