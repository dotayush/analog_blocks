\section{Active}
Active filters replace inductors by op-amp feedback
(Section~\ref{sec:devices_opamp}). All are derived with KCL in Q4 with an
ideal op-amp (\(V_+ = V_-\), no input current); the finite gain-bandwidth
must exceed the filter's \(f_0\) times its gain by a comfortable margin
(\(GB > 10\,|H(f_0)|\,f_0\), and \(GB > 100\,Q\,f_0\) for high \(Q\)).
