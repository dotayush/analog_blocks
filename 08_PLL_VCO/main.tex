\chapter{PLLs and VCOs}\label{ch:pll_vco}

A Voltage Controlled Oscillator (VCO) converts a control voltage into a
frequency. A Phase Locked Loop (PLL) wraps a VCO in negative feedback so
that the phase of its output tracks the phase of a reference. The VCOs are
the oscillators; the PLL is the control system around them (analysed in
Q4 as a linear loop in phase, once locked).
