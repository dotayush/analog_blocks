\chapter{Filters}
\label{ch:filters}

This chapter covers various types of electronic filters,
which are circuits designed to selectively allow or block
certain frequency components of a signal.

\subsubsection{Non-Idealities}
All filters are subject to non-idealities that can affect
their performance. Some common non-idealities include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Component Tolerances:} Refer to \ref{sec:theory_general_tolerances}.
  \item \textbf{Parasitics:} Refer to \ref{sec:theory_general_parasitics}.
  \item \textbf{Loading Effects:} Refer to \ref{sec:theory_general_loading}.
\end{itemize}
