\chapter{Amplifiers}\label{ch:amplifiers}

An amplifier scales a signal by a gain \(A\). Blocks here are divided by
what we look at when we use them:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Common:} single transistor stages named after the
    terminal shared by input and output (common source, gate, drain,
    emitter, base, collector).
  \item \textbf{Functional:} operational amplifier usage, named after the
    function the closed loop performs (buffer, inverting, summing,
    integrating, \ldots).
  \item \textbf{Differential:} the operational amplifier itself, built
    from differential pairs, mirrors and cascodes.
\end{enumerate}
