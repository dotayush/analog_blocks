\section{Passives}

It is not necessary but it is common to find many two port devices are
passive in nature. Once placed in a circuit, they will start working
automatically as they do not require any external power source to operate.
These devices are ideally linear and time-invariant although in practice,
they are not (for example, a device property can change with temperature,
age, and even the voltage or current interacting with it).
