\chapter{Devices \& Basic Blocks}

A device is a physical component that can be used to build circuits.
Devices can be classified based on their functionality, characteristics,
and behavior. In the context of analog circuits, devices are
primarily classified into two categories: passive and active devices.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Passive Devices: These devices do not require an external
      power source to operate. They can store or dissipate energy
      but cannot generate it. Examples of passive devices include
      resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers.
    \item Active Devices: These devices require an external power
      source to operate. They can amplify signals, control current
      flow, and perform other functions that passive devices cannot.
      Examples of active devices include transistors, and
      operational amplifiers.
\end{itemize}
