Every symbol and label that appears in this chapter.
\begin{symtable}
\(V_{cc},\ V_{ee}\) & the two driving voltage sources at the ends of a voltage divider & V \\ \hline
\(V_{out}\) & divider output node voltage & V \\ \hline
\(V_{out_1},\ V_{out_2}\) & output due to \(V_{cc}\) alone and due to \(V_{ee}\) alone (superposition) & V \\ \hline
\(I_{in}\) & current driven into a current divider & A \\ \hline
\(V_{in}\) & voltage of the shared node of a current divider (Node) & V \\ \hline
\(Node\) & the node shared by the two branches of a current divider & - \\ \hline
\(I_{out}\) & output (branch) current & A \\ \hline
\(I_{G_1}, I_{G_2},\ I_{C_1}, I_{C_2},\ I_{L_1}, I_{L_2}\) & branch current through \(G_1, G_2\), \(C_1, C_2\), \(L_1, L_2\) & A \\ \hline
\(G_1, G_2,\ R_1, R_2\) & the two conductances and resistances \(R_k = \frac{1}{G_k}\) & S, \(\Omega\) \\ \hline
\(C_1, C_2,\ C_{eq}\) & the two capacitors and their equivalent (series or parallel, as stated) & F \\ \hline
\(L_1, L_2\) & the two inductors & H \\ \hline
\(H_1(s), H_2(s)\) & transfer function from each source (or to each branch) & - \\ \hline
\(N_1, N_2,\ D_1, D_2\) & numerator and denominator of \(H_1\), \(H_2\) & - \\ \hline
\(Z_{in},\ Z_{out}\) & input and output impedance & \(\Omega\) \\ \hline
\(Z_L,\ R_L\) & load impedance and load resistance & \(\Omega\) \\ \hline
\(s,\ j,\ \omega,\ f,\ \pi\) & Laplace variable, \(\sqrt{-1}\), angular frequency \(2\pi f\), frequency, pi & rad/s, -, rad/s, Hz, - \\ \hline
\(sC,\ sL\) & admittance of a capacitor, impedance of an inductor & S, \(\Omega\) \\ \hline
\(k\Omega,\ \mu F,\ nF,\ nH\) & unit prefixes kilo (\(10^3\)), micro (\(10^{-6}\)), nano (\(10^{-9}\)) & - \\ \hline
\(R\) & a resistance in general & \(\Omega\) \\ \hline
\(sC_1, sC_2, sC_{eq},\ sL_1, sL_2\) & admittance of each capacitor and impedance of each inductor & S, \(\Omega\) \\ \hline
\end{symtable}
