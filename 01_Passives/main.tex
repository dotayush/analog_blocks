\chapter{Passives}

This chapter covers fundamental passive building blocks
built from resistors, capacitors, inductors. The blocks
perform a special function which makes them hard to group
in a single category. The blocks are widely used in
analog circuit design.

\section{Resistors, Capacitors and Inductors}
These two port devices are the fundamental building blocks of
all analog circuits. They are called passive devices as they do
not require any external power source to operate.
