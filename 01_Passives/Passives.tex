\chapter{Passives}

This chapter contains circuits that provide a special
function and are made up of only passive components.
Passive components or circuits are those that do not
supply energy to the signal path and thus can only
attenuate signals. They do not require any external
power supply to operate.

A few examples of passive components are resistors,
capacitors, inductors, transformers etc.
