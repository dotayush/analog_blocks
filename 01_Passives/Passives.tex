\chapter{Passives}

\section{Overview}
This chapter covers fundamental passive building blocks
built from resistors, capacitors, inductors that perform
a special function, are general enough to not belong to
a specific category and are widely used in analog circuit
design.

\section{Behavior}
It's hard to classify the behavior here due to the
diversity of building blocks within this chapter. If
required to be known, each building block will have
its own behavior section.

\section{Usages}
Similar to behavior, usages are specific to each building
block. Each building block will have its own usages
section if required.
